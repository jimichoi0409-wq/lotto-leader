## 1부. 비공개 테스트

**Q1. 테스터를 어떻게 모집했나요?** (How did you recruit users for your closed test?)
> I invited friends, family and acquaintances who regularly play lotteries (Korea, Vietnam, US and Europe) by email and messenger, and added them to an email list in Play Console. 13 people were invited and 12 opted in.

**Q2. 테스터 모집이 얼마나 쉬웠나요?** → 선택지 중 "보통(Neither easy nor difficult)" 권장

**Q3. 테스트 기간 동안 테스터 참여도를 설명하세요.** (Describe the engagement you received from testers)
> 12 testers stayed opted in for the full period. 12 of the 13 devices that installed the app opened it and monthly active devices reached 13. Testers generated number combinations with different filter settings, checked results of several lotteries (Korea Lotto 6/45, Vietnam Mega 6/45, Powerball, EuroMillions, etc.), used the history/win-check screen and tried the draw-day reminders. Crash rate during the test was zero.

**Q4. 받은 의견을 요약하세요.** (Summary of feedback)
> Testers said the pattern filters and the live "remaining combinations" count were easy to understand. Feedback included: the app icon looked small on the home screen, some users wanted more lotteries and languages, and some draw results arrived late for certain countries.

## 2부. 앱 정보

**Q5. 대상 사용자는 누구인가요?** (Who is the intended audience?)
> Adults (18+) who play national lotteries and want a simple tool to create number combinations with their own rules, check past results and statistics, and get draw reminders. The app supports 11 lotteries in 8 languages.

**Q6. 앱이 사용자에게 어떤 가치를 제공하나요?** (How does your app provide value?)
> Lotto Leader lets players exclude patterns they don't want (consecutive numbers, all odd/even, past winning combinations, etc.) using up to 24 filters, saves their combinations and automatically checks them against official results. It clearly states that every combination has the same odds and that the app does not sell tickets or offer betting or cash rewards.

**Q7. 첫해 예상 설치 수** → 선택지 중 "10,000 미만" 또는 "1만~10만" (현실적으로 낮은 구간 권장)

## 3부. 프로덕션 준비

**Q8. 테스트에서 배운 점을 바탕으로 무엇을 바꿨나요?** (Changes made based on the closed test)
> - Enlarged the app icon artwork by 20% for better visibility (version 6).
> - Replaced one lottery whose data source was not reliable and added three new lotteries (France Loto, Japan Loto 7, Brazil Mega-Sena) with licensed data sources, for 11 lotteries in total.
> - Added Portuguese (Brazil) for 8 languages.
> - Improved handling of late or irregular draws (manual entry and stale-data checks) so results stay up to date.
> - Fixed a layout error on the onboarding screen.

**Q9. 프로덕션 준비가 되었다고 판단한 근거** (How did you decide your app is ready for production?)
> The closed test ran for 14+ days with 12 opted-in testers, Android vitals show no crashes or ANRs, all policy declarations are complete with no policy issues, and the tester feedback above has been addressed in the latest release.
